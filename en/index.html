aMule Remote privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Leer en español

This policy explains what data the aMule Remote application (the “app”) processes, why, and what your rights are. The app is a remote control for your own aMule server: downloads are done by your server, not by the phone.

Controller

Appsconarte, the app developer. Contact: infodeicer@gmail.com

What we do NOT do

  • We have no servers of our own that receive your data or your aMule traffic.
  • We do not require registration or an account: the Google account is optional.
  • We never store your aMule password in the cloud or on any server of ours.
  • We do not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

1. Connection to your aMule

The app connects directly, from your phone, to the address (IP or name), port and password you type in. This traffic goes only between your phone and your aMule server; it does not pass through us. The password itself is not stored: the app only keeps its fingerprint (MD5) on your phone, which is what the aMule protocol uses to log in, and sends it only to your own server.

2. Data stored on your phone

The app stores only on your device: the name, address and port of your server, the password fingerprint, any ed2k links you choose to save, your preferences, and whether you bought “Remove ads”. None of this data reaches us.

3. Google account (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app uses Firebase (Google) to store in the cloud only the name you gave your server, its address (IP or name) and its port, linked to your account, so you can recover them on another phone. Not stored: your aMule password or its fingerprint, your downloads, or your links. You can delete this data at any time from the app (“Delete my cloud data”) and sign out. If you do not sign in, nothing is sent to the cloud.

4. Advertising

The app is free thanks to a small advertising banner. Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited), which may process your device's advertising ID, IP address, technical device data and your interactions with ads in order to show them, measure them and prevent fraud.

The first time you open the app we ask for your consent through Google's form. If you do not consent, the ads you see will be non-personalised. You can change your choice in the app, under More › Support the app › Ad privacy, and reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings. More information: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

5. Purchases and donations

“Remove ads” and voluntary donations are paid through Google Play. Google handles the payment under its own terms; we do not receive your payment details, only confirmation that the purchase exists.

Legal basis

Personalised advertising is based on your consent (GDPR art. 6.1.a), which you can withdraw at any time. The optional account is based on your express request to use it (art. 6.1.b) and purchase handling on performance of a contract (art. 6.1.b).

How to delete your aMule Remote data

  1. Cloud account data: in the app, More › Google account › Delete my cloud data. The saved name, address and port are removed immediately.
  2. Everything stored on the phone: in Android, Settings › Apps › aMule Remote › Storage › Clear data, or uninstall the app.
  3. Advertising data: in the app, Ad privacy to withdraw your consent, and in Android, Settings › Privacy › Ads › Delete advertising ID. Data already processed by Google AdMob is kept according to Google's policy (policies.google.com/technologies/retention).
  4. If you prefer to ask us: write to infodeicer@gmail.com with the subject “Delete data – aMule Remote”. We will reply within 30 days.

What is kept: nothing in our systems after the cloud data is deleted. Your purchases are recorded in your Google Play account, which keeps them under its own policy so you can restore them if you reinstall the app.

Children

The app is not aimed at children under 13.

Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability by writing to infodeicer@gmail.com. For advertising and purchase data you can also contact Google. You may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or your local authority.
